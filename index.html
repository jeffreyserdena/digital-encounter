    Digital Encounter site review: scope and limits. Each page is reviewed from screenshots at 1280 px (desktop) and 390 px (mobile) plus its text, and up to 25 internal links are checked. The tool does not log in, submit forms, test bookings or measure real loading speed, and it does not certify accessibility, security or legal compliance. Long pages are captured only up to the first five desktop and three mobile sections. Findings written by the AI are suggestions to check, not verdicts; items marked Validate need the owner’s confirmation.
